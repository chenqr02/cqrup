I am **Qirui Chen**, a Ph.D. student at the **University of Science and Technology of China (USTC)**. My education includes an M.S. at USTC from 2024 to 2026 and undergraduate study at Hefei University of Technology from 2020 to 2024.

Outside my degree program, I publish as **低级攻城师** on Bilibili. My channel bio says “智力低下.” On 2026-09-20, I had posted 58 videos and one article, built a 17-entry “生活流水账” series, and gathered 84 followers. I report the follower count as a platform metric, not as a statistically significant sample.

I have kept the standard academic sections on this homepage, but my active publication record is mostly video: travel logs, tastings, music, campus events, and occasional gaming streams.

### Research interests

- **Field sites:** I investigate everyday life, food, travel, and events.
- **Methods:** I record first, edit later, and treat video duration as a measure of commitment rather than quality.
- **Open question:** how much of a trip can I document before it becomes a longitudinal study? My Yunnan video currently runs 7 hours, 38 minutes, and 33 seconds.

You can find me on [GitHub](https://github.com/chenqr02) and [Bilibili](https://space.bilibili.com/511528679). I trace my content timeline on [Creator Path]({{ '/creator-path/' | relative_url }}) and list my videos under [Publications]({{ '/publications/' | relative_url }}).
